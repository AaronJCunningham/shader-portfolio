## Concept

**"Letter to No One"** — A sigil form emerges from the void like a message written in automatic drawing, half-heard. One axis of symmetry (the body, the hand, the desire) — but broken by the subconscious, interrupted, left incomplete. The geometry is a torus distorted by displacement, as if the shape were being drawn by a hand that doesn't know it's drawing. Bone-white against absolute black. Tenderness made visible: a form reaching toward something that cannot receive it.

The torus is the only primitive. It twists, displaces, fractures along its axis, reforms. It breathes. It is the alphabet of desire rendered in pure geometry — each distortion a letter, each fracture a word.

## Visual & Rendering Spec

### Scene Setup
- **Camera:** Perspective, fixed at z=5, looking at origin. Subtle slow drift (0.05 rad/s orbit around Y).
- **Lighting:** No traditional lights — emission only. The bone-white is self-illuminating against the void.
- **Environment:** Pure black background (#000000). No fog, no skybox. The void is absolute.

### Geometry
- **Single primitive:** TorusGeometry(1.0, 0.3, 64, 128)
- The torus is the sigil-glyph — twisting along its major axis, displacement-driven distortion to break the symmetry.
- Major axis rotation: slow continuous spin (0.1 rad/s around Y), minor axis oscillation (sine-driven wobble).

### Materials & Effects
- **Material:** MeshStandardMaterial with emissive bone-white (#c8b8a2), metalness 0.1, roughness 0.8.
- **Post-processing:** Subtle Bloom (threshold 0.8, strength 0.3, radius 0.5). The glow is not harsh — it's the faint luminescence of bone in darkness.
- **Displacement:** Vertex shader displacement using layered sine waves + simplex noise. As time increases, displacement amplitude grows, peaks, then recedes — the sigil forming and dissolving.

### Color Application
- Background: #000000 (void)
- Torus base: #c8b8a2 (bone)
- Emissive highlights: #e8ddd0 (warm bone)
- Deep shadows: #111111 (near-black crevices)

## Implementation Notes

- **Single primitive only.** No spheres, no boxes, no planes.
- **Fragment shader** for the bone-void aesthetic — no texture maps needed, pure procedural.
- **Time-driven animation** using `useFrame` — no audio, no interaction required.
- The displacement should feel like the automatic line — dense, organic, as if the hand was moving without intent.
