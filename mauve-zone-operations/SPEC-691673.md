## 2. Concept

**"The hand moves. Something reaches."**

A system of concentric rings — all that exists — breathing in and out like the chest of something sleeping. The rings are *not* precise. They are drawn by a hand that doesn't know what it's making. Edges are rough, spacing irregular, as if each ring is a different breath drawn by a different impulse.

The "hunger" is expressed as a subtle centripetal distortion — all the rings lean slightly inward, as if reaching toward a point that offers nothing. An attractor with no object. The longing that has nothing to attach to.

The piece exists in the moment before recognition. The rings almost form something — a sigil, a face, an atavistic shape — then drift apart. Resolution is threatened but never delivered. The viewer leans in. Nothing happens. The hunger remains hungry.

**Single primitive:** `TorusGeometry` (thin tube) — scaled, distorted, layered. One primitive. Everything from that.

## 3. Visual & Rendering Specification

### Scene Setup
- **Camera:** PerspectiveCamera, FOV 60, positioned at z=5, looking at origin
- **Controls:** None — fully autonomous, self-animating
- **Background:** `#050505` (near-black void)
- **Fog:** `FogExp2`, color `#050505`, density `0.15`

### Rings
- **Count:** 20 concentric rings
- **Geometry:** `TorusGeometry(r=1.5, tube=0.008, radialSegments=128, tubularSegments=1)` — very thin wire-like rings
- **Base radius:** Logarithmic spacing — inner tight, outer loose
- **Spacing jitter:** Each ring's radius offset by a random delta, recalculated every ~10s with easing — the spacing breathes
- **Scale breathing:** Each ring pulses scale on its own phase offset — `0.94` to `1.06x`, sin/cos driven, ~6s period per ring
- **Edge roughness:** Vertex displacement in ring plane using low-frequency value noise — rings feel hand-drawn, not machined
- **Rotation:** Each ring rotates slowly on Z axis, individual speeds, some clockwise some counter-clockwise
- **Color mapping:** Inner rings `#e0e0e0`, transitioning through `#888888`, `#444444`, outer rings `#1a1a1a`

### The Hunger (Centripetal Distortion)
- Virtual attractor at world origin — no visual representation
- Vertex displacement toward center: `strength * 1/(distance + epsilon)` — the closer to center, the more pull
- Very subtle — barely perceptible as distortion, felt as presence

### The Almost-Moment
- Every 20–30s (randomized interval): ring spacing tightens toward a sigil-adjacent ratio
- Holds for ~1.5 seconds — rings almost resolve into a recognizable form
- Then drifts apart — the resolution retreats
- The viewer cannot hold what they almost saw

### Post-Processing
- **Bloom:** `UnrealBloomPass`, threshold `0.15`, strength `0.5`, radius `0.6`
- **Film grain:** Noise-based grain overlay at `opacity 0.04`
