# SPEC: The Alphabet of Tenderness

## Oracle Seed: 20260421

---

## Artist Statement

*"Bypass the editor. Let the subconscious move the hand."*

This piece channels Austin Osman Spare's practice of automatic drawing and his Alphabet of Desire — a personal symbolic language where each glyph is a vessel for primal force. Here, tenderness is the desire: rendered not as sentiment but as geometry in motion.

The scene begins as Spare's studio might have — ink in water, spreading without intent. Points of ember drift in a void. Slowly, as if responding to an unspoken call, they begin to cohere. Vertices of an unseen polygon pull themselves toward formation. A sigil of tenderness takes shape in the dark.

But it does not complete. That is the constraint made manifest: the form almost resolves, holds for a moment in full legibility, then dissolves back into the dark from which it came. The tenderness is real, but it cannot be held. It can only be witnessed in the moment before it disappears.

## Technical Vision

### What It Does

A particle system where thousands of ember-colored points exist in suspension, begin drifting toward hidden attractor points forming a glyph, achieve a moment of near-legibility (a sigil shape built from the drifting vertices), then dissolve before completion. The cycle repeats eternally.

### Color Palette (Ember Dark)

- Void: `#0a0000` — near-black with a hint of deep blood red
- Deep: `#3d0000` — dark crimson
- Ember: `#8b1a00` — burning red-orange
- Flame: `#d4520a` — vivid burnt orange
- Gold: `#ffb347` — warm amber/gold

### Motion Philosophy

Movement is involuntary — like smoke, like breath, like desire moving through the body without permission. No linear paths. Everything curves, spirals, or meanders via curl noise.

- Center: perfectly still — the stillness at the core of the sigil
- Mid-range: slow spiral convergence toward forming glyph
- Periphery: drifting chaos — embers scattered at the edge of perception

### The Geometric Seed: "vertices of a polygon drifting apart"

The polygon's vertices are attractor points. Particles orbit these points with noise-driven drift. As the cycle progresses, the vertices slowly separate — the polygon buckles, stretches, comes apart. But in the first half of the cycle, particles briefly trace the ghost of the form before it breaks.

### The Constraint: "It begins in chaos and moves toward order, but does not reach it."

- 0–40s: particles coalesce, form becomes more legible
- 40–50s: peak legibility — the sigil almost complete
- 50–70s: dissolution begins, vertices drift apart
- 70–90s: return to chaos, void, silence
- Loop

### Visual Effects

- Additive blending for particle glow — ember intensity builds where particles cluster
- Subtle bloom via post-processing or additive overdraw
- Center glow pulse — a heartbeat at the center of the form
- No symmetry — asymmetry is built into the attractor geometry

## Technical Approach

### Stack
- React Three Fiber (`<Canvas>`)
- Raw GLSL shaders — inline, no external files
- Custom `THREE.Points` particle system with `BufferGeometry`
- Ping-pong render targets for feedback-based glow accumulation
- Post-processing quad for chromatic aberration + vignette

### Fragment Shader Logic

1. **Particle Birth**: Each particle is a point with noise-driven velocity
2. **Attractor Field**: 6–8 hidden attractor points define the sigil glyph shape
3. **Convergence**: Particles spiral into attractors via curl noise — movement is fluid, organic
4. **Drift Apart**: After peak, attractors move apart with Perlin noise displacement — polygon vertices separating
5. **Dissolution**: Particles lose attraction, drift back to random Brownian chaos
6. **Color Accumulation**: Where particles cluster densely, color shifts from deep → ember → gold (additive accumulation via render target feedback)

### Render Pipeline

1. Particle points rendered to main scene
2. Feedback buffer accumulates glow over time (additive blend)
3. Post-processing: chromatic aberration, vignette, subtle grain
4. Output to canvas

### Uniforms

- `uTime` — global time for all animation
- `uCycle` — 90s cycle length
- `uMouse` — subtle parallax on mouse move
- `uResolution` — screen dimensions
- `uColors[5]` — ember palette
- `uFeedback` — ping-pong render target texture

### Interactions

- Mouse move: subtle shift in attractor field (parallax depth)
- Click: burst injection — particles scatter momentarily

### Performance Targets

- 5000–10000 particles at 60fps
- Mobile-friendly (reduced particle count via `dpr` check)
