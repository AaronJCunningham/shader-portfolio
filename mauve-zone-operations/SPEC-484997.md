## 2. Concept

**"Something holds still at the center of the void. The edges keep arriving."**

Steffi Grant's glyphs were not designed — they were *received*. Precise transmissions from the nightside, assembled at the edge of consciousness. The center of her symbols never wavers. The complexity lives in the perimeter.

This scene is the inverse of Hunger Circuit. Where that piece was rings *reaching inward*, this is rings *assembling outward* — arriving from the dark, fixing into a perfect glyph at the exact center, then continuing their slow rotation at the periphery while the core holds absolute stillness.

Nu-Isis: the cosmic feminine void. Not emptiness — *receptivity*. The center of the composition is the still point of a transmission device. The rings are the signal arriving. The catastrophe is always about to happen at the edge and never does.

Calm. Precise. Strange.

## 3. Visual & Rendering Specification

### Scene Setup
- **Camera:** PerspectiveCamera, FOV 55, positioned at z=6, looking at origin
- **Controls:** None — fully autonomous
- **Background:** `#050505` (void black)
- **Fog:** `FogExp2`, color `#050505`, density `0.08`

### Rings — The Glyph Machine
- **Count:** 24 concentric rings
- **Geometry:** `TorusGeometry(r=1.0, tube=0.006, radialSegments=256, tubularSegments=1)` — hair-thin, precise
- **Radius distribution:** Logarithmic spacing, outermost ring at r=4.5, innermost at r=0.08
- **Spacing:** Irregular — radius deltas jittered by random ±0.03–0.12 each frame (subtle micro-drift), recalculated on long cycle (~15s)
- **Color:** Inner rings `#e0e0e0`, mid `#888888`, outer `#444444`, outermost edge `#1a1a1a`

### The Still Center — Nu-Isis
- Inner 4 rings (r < 0.5) have **zero rotation, zero drift, zero breathing** — absolute stillness
- These 4 rings are the *glyph* — a precise, fixed geometric figure at the heart of the scene
- A single `PlaneGeometry` disc at center, radius 0.05, color `#e0e0e0`, opacity 0.3 — the eye of the transmission
- The stillness of the center is the *point* — it is what is being received

### Peripheral Motion
- Rings 5–24 rotate on Z axis: inner rings slow, outer rings faster (gradient from 0.0003 to 0.003 rad/frame)
- Direction: outer rings clockwise, inner of the moving set counter-clockwise — opposing currents
- **Breathing at edge:** Outermost 8 rings pulse scale ±4%, each on individual phase, ~8s period
- **Arrival drift:** Every ~25–35s, the outermost 3 rings briefly drift *inward* as if something is about to complete — then release back to their orbit

### Precision Disruption
- Vertex-level perturbation on the tube surface: high-frequency sine wave displacement along tube normal, amplitude 0.002 — barely visible as texture, felt as *life*
- This gives the precision geometry a hand-drawn quality — Steffi's trained hand, not a machine

### Post-Processing
- **Bloom:** `UnrealBloomPass`, threshold `0.2`, strength `0.4`, radius `0.7`
- **Film grain:** `Noise` overlay, opacity `0.035`
- **Vignette:** Subtle darkening at corners, strength `0.3`
