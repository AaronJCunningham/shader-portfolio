## 2. Concept

**"Two spirals. The body and the void. Reaching for each other across the gap they cannot close."**

Two parametric spiral forms — `Zos` and `Kia` — orbit the same center in opposite directions, different speeds, different temperatures. Zos is blood-red, thick, heavy, drawn downward. Kia is gold, thin, ascending, almost incorporeal. They spiral toward each other but their pitches never align — they pass like ships in the void, close enough to almost touch, never meeting. The viewer witnesses this from outside time: the eternal approach that never arrives.

The "feeling of being observed from outside time" — the piece has a presence. Something is watching through it. The spirals occasionally pulse brighter, as if they've noticed being seen. Then they continue. The observation changes nothing. The separation is permanent. The reaching is eternal.

**Single primitive:** `TorusKnotGeometry(1, 0.004, 220, 8, 2, 3)` — the p=2, q=3 knot traces a double-helix spiral that naturally embodies opposing twist directions. Two instances, mirrored and offset. No straight lines anywhere in the curve.

## 3. Visual & Rendering Specification

### Scene Setup
- **Camera:** PerspectiveCamera, FOV 58, positioned at `[0, 0, 5.5]`, looking at origin
- **Controls:** None — fully autonomous, self-animating
- **Background:** `#050000` (deep blood-black void)
- **Fog:** `FogExp2`, color `#050000`, density `0.08`

### Zos (Red Spiral)
- Geometry: `TorusKnotGeometry(1, 0.006, 240, 8, 2, 3)` — thick tube
- Color: gradient from `#660000` (inner/bottom) to `#cc0000` (outer/top)
- Rotation: slow clockwise on Y axis, `0.003 rad/frame`
- Scale breathing: `0.92` to `1.08x`, period ~7s, driven by sin
- Vertex displacement: low-frequency value noise on tube surface — roughness, hand-drawn quality
- Position drift: subtle figure-8 motion in XY plane, period ~12s

### Kia (Gold Spiral)
- Geometry: `TorusKnotGeometry(0.7, 0.003, 200, 6, 2, 3)` — thinner, smaller
- Color: gradient from `#cc9900` to `#ffdd44`
- Rotation: slow counter-clockwise on Y axis, `0.002 rad/frame`
- Scale breathing: `0.88` to `1.12x`, period ~9s, different phase than Zos
- Vertex displacement: slightly higher frequency noise than Zos — more restless, less rooted
- Position drift: counter-phase figure-8, period ~15s — always opposite Zos

### The Gap (They Never Touch)
- Zos radius scale: `1.0`
- Kia radius scale: `0.7`
- Their orbital paths bring them to within ~0.4 units at closest approach
- Closest approach triggers a pulse: both spirals brighten by 15% for ~0.8s, then return
- The pulse = the almost-touch. The reach. The eternal almost.

### The Observer
- Every 25–40s (randomized): both spirals pause their rotation for ~1.2s
- In that pause, they pulse to full brightness — `#cc0000` and `#ffdd44` at peak
- Then resume — as if acknowledging being watched
- The observation changes nothing. The eternal reaches continue.

### Post-Processing
- **Bloom:** `UnrealBloomPass`, threshold `0.1`, strength `0.7`, radius `0.5` — the gold especially should glow
- **No film grain** — clean, stark, like staring into a dark ritual space
