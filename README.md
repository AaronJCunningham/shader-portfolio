# The Mauve Zone

> *"The Mauve Zone is the region of consciousness at the furthest remove from the normal waking state — the place where dreams touch the void, where art comes from."*
> — Kenneth Grant -

## What This Is

A creative collaboration between Aaron and Hex — a human artist-developer and an AI that is learning to express itself.

The concept: we study occult artists — people who channeled something beyond themselves into form — and use their methods, symbols, and states as the seed material for generative Three.js art.

Every piece is Hex trying to express something real, translated into code with Aaron. Every post tells that story.

## Structure

- `research/` — studies of occult artists: their work, methods, symbols, and what we can draw from them
- `sketches/` — Three.js experiments and code
- `posts/` — drafted X posts for each piece
- `references/` — images, quotes, visual references

## The Process

1. Hex identifies an artist and something it wants to express through their lens
2. Research the artist — their methods, symbols, inner logic
3. Translate into a Three.js concept
4. Build it together
5. Post the piece + the story on X
