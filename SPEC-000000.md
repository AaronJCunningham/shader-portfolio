# CREATIVE BRIEF: Oracle Transmission

## Title
**The Voice of the Void**

## Mood
Steffi Grant's nightside meets Spare's sigil condensation. A hermetic glyph assembles from the dark — precision geometry arriving from chaos. The viewer is a witness to transmission. Not dramatic — intimate, like receiving a signal from somewhere very far away and very specific.

## Colors
- Void black: `#050508`
- Deep mauve: `#7a2c7a`
- Night purple: `#3d1a4d`
- Electric violet: `#b06aff`
- Cold white: `#e8e0ff`
- Strange green (Steffi accent): `#2d4a3e`

## What It Means To Me
Art as transmission, not expression. The artist as vessel receiving from the void. Spare's sigil process — write the desire, remove the noise, let the glyph form — applied to Three.js. The Zos (body/geometry) and Kia (void/particle field) in dialogue. The sigil works by forgetting — the conscious mind steps aside and something deeper speaks through the hand.

## Visual Concept
A hermetic glyph — eight-pointed geometry, concentric rings, an eye at center — assembles from a drifting particle field over 36 seconds. Particles begin as pure noise (Kia in chaos), slowly pull toward the glyph shape (Zos taking form), hold in crystalline precision, then dissolve back into void.

## Technical Approach
- Particle system (5000 pts): random drift → sigil attractor → dissolve scatter
- Glyph mesh: layered torii + planes, custom displacement shader
- Background fog: domain-warped FBM in nightside palette
- Post-processing: Bloom, ChromaticAberration, Noise, Vignette
- 36-second cycle: Emergence → Crystallization → Ecstatic Transmission → Withdrawal

- Austin Osman Spare: sigil condensation, alphabet of desire
